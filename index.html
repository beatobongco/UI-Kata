#26: Pyramid

A mobile-first board climbing tracker for building a send pyramid over time. Inspired by Hevy and Slay the Spire: log attempts/sends with one tap, rest by grade, compare against your last session, and slowly move your climbing base upward. Offline-first, no login, local-only data.

#25: Lightcycles 2

A modern remake of a Tron-inspired competitive snake game. Meticulously tuned playing against friends to be e-sports lite. Super fun to play arcade-style over beers. Has Arcade mode, multiple AI enemies, 2 player local and online versus.

#24: PickleQ

PickleQ helps pickleball open play organizers check players in, rotate courts fairly, and cut down the constant "who's up next?" interruptions with a live queue players can follow from their phones. As of April 2026, it is used by hundreds of venues and thousands of players daily.
